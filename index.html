CONCEPT ILLUSTRATION
02 · Course project / Regression

Predicting power plant output

Used 9,568 hourly observations from the Combined Cycle Power Plant dataset to explore how environmental conditions relate to electrical energy output.

pandasscikit-learnRegression
Behind the project ↗

I explored temperature, ambient pressure, relative humidity and exhaust vacuum, then compared a Linear Regression baseline with a Random Forest Regressor. Model evaluation used RMSE, MAE and R², alongside plots to interpret the results.
